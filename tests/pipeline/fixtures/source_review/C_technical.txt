Title: Motion and Algorithms

## Newton's Second Law
Force is defined as the product of mass and acceleration.

F = m × a

where F is the force in newtons and m is the mass in kilograms.

Example: a 2 kg ball pushed with a force of 10 N accelerates at 5 m/s².

## Kinetic Energy
Kinetic energy is the energy an object has because of its motion.

KE = ½ m v²

## Computing the Area of a Circle
The following Python function returns the area of a circle for a given radius.

```python
def area(r):
    return 3.14159 * r * r

print(area(2))
```

Output: 12.56636

## Binary Search
Binary search is an algorithm that finds a value in a sorted list by repeatedly halving the search range.
1. Compare the target with the middle element.
2. If they are equal, the search ends.
3. Otherwise continue in the half that can contain the target.
