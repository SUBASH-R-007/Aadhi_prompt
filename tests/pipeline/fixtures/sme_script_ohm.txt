SEGMENT 2 - OHM'S LAW [0:40 - 1:50]
BOARD Title: OHM'S LAW
Aadhi speaks:
Ohm's law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided the temperature stays constant.
In symbols, V = I x R, where V is the voltage in volts, I is the current in amperes and R is the resistance in ohms.
The law was published by Georg Simon Ohm in 1827.
BOARD displays - formula / expression block:
V = I x R
ANIMATION: A battery pushes glowing charges through a resistor; doubling the battery doubles the flow.
DETAILED ANIMATION / VISUAL:
Show a simple circuit with a cell and a resistor. Increase the voltage in two steps and show the ammeter reading rise in proportion.

SEGMENT 3 - WORKED EXAMPLE (1:50 - 2:40)
Aadhi speaks:
A 12 V battery is connected across a 4 ohm resistor. The current is I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 A.
The duration of the pulse in the next experiment is 2 ms, so the charge delivered is only 6 mC.

SEGMENT 2 - ELECTRICAL POWER [0:20 - 1:30]
Aadhi speaks:
Electrical power is the rate at which electrical energy is converted. It is measured in watts.
For any device, P = V x I. Using Ohm's law, P = I^2 x R = V^2 / R.
ANIMATION: A geyser and an LED lamp side by side; the geyser's power meter spins much faster.
